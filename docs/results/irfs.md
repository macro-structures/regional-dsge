
# Dynamic Responses to an Innovation-Cost Reduction

Impulse response functions (IRFs) illustrate how the
three-region DSGE model responds to a reduction in
innovation costs, holding other structural shocks at zero.

The analysis compares the dynamic responses of North,
South and Wales.

## 3. Interpretation and Policy Implications

These impulse responses are conditional on the
model's structural parameters, shock specification,
and calibration.

The quantitative interpretation requires identifying
the magnitude and persistence of the innovation-cost
shock, the transformations of the variables, and
the regional aggregation weights.

The results should not automatically be interpreted
as equal-fiscal-cost policy effects.
