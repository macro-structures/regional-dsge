# Model Results

This section reports preliminary quantitative results from the three-region North–South–Wales dynamic stochastic general equilibrium (DSGE) model. Its purpose is to distinguish estimation, model validation, shock accounting, and policy counterfactuals.

| Research question | Evidence | Page |
|---|---|---|
| How are structural parameters estimated? | Indirect inference | [Estimation](estimation.md) |
| Is the model statistically consistent with the chosen auxiliary statistics? | Wald specification test | [Specification tests](specification.md) |
| Can the test detect economically meaningful departures from the null? | Monte Carlo power | [Power analysis](power.md) |
| Which shocks explain regional volatility and historical dynamics? | FEVD and historical decomposition | [Decompositions](decomposition.md) |
| How do regions respond to an innovation-cost shock? | Impulse response functions | [Dynamic responses](irfs.md) |

**Research status.** Figures are from a working research model and are not causal estimates of implemented policies. Auxiliary-model specifications, calibration, uncertainty intervals and reproducibility checks should accompany the final academic release.

**Interpretation.** A specification-test failure to reject is not proof that the structural model is correct. FEVD is model-conditional attribution, not a causal policy effect; IRFs are responses to model shocks, not empirical forecasts.
