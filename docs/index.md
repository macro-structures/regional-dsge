# Regional DSGE

Understanding Regional economy does not require persuasive narratives or ad-hoc explanations.  
It requires a structural framework.

Regional economy is analysed through a structural DSGE framework.

The framework is evaluated against three criteria:

1. Existence and stability of equilibrium solutions  
2. Bayesian-type evaluation and feasible parameters estimated  
3. Empirical credibility via indirect inference (II)

The purpose is not prediction, but stress-testing mechanisms
and identifying regions of fragility in policy design.
