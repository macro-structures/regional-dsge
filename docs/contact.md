## Research Collaboration & Policy Engagement

I welcome enquiries and opportunities for academic collaboration, particularly in regional macroeconomics, productivity, innovation, structural modelling, and economic policy evaluation.

My ongoing research explores how national and devolved policies affect regional economic performance across the United Kingdom, with a particular focus on Wales.

**Feedback and policy simulation proposals are especially welcome.** If you have identified a potential issue with my simulations, would like to discuss the findings, or wish to explore a particular policy scenario, please feel free to get in touch.

I would be pleased to engage with researchers, policymakers, and other interested parties.
