Regional DSGE

Structural analysis of regional economies using DSGE models

This site presents a structural framework for analysing regional economic dynamics using multi-region DSGE models. Understanding Regional economy does not require persuasive narratives or ad-hoc explanations. It requires a structural framework. Regional economy is analysed through a structural DSGE framework. The framework is evaluated against three criteria: 1.Existence and stability of equilibrium solutions 2.Bayesian-type evaluation and feasible parameters estimated 3.Empirical credibility via indirect inference (II) The purpose is not prediction, but stress-testing mechanisms and identifying regions of fragility in policy design.

Focus

  • North–South–Wales three-region DSGE models
  • Productivity, innovation, and regional divergence
  • Policy evaluation under structural uncertainty

Notes

The objective is not forecasting, but understanding structural mechanisms and stress-testing policy designs.
