## Current Research

### Regional Inequality and Policy Transmission in the UK

My doctoral research develops a three-region DSGE
model of the UK, distinguishing North, South and Wales.

The model investigates regional disparities,
innovation, productivity and the transmission of
national and regional economic policies.
